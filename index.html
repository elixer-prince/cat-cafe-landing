The residents

Twelve rescues, and six of them run the place.

Every cat at Catpuccino arrived through Camden Cat Rescue. They set their own schedules, choose their own sofas, and are never woken up for a photo. Here are the six most likely to greet you.

  • Marmalade Maine Coon

    Claims the window table at 9am sharp and supervises the espresso machine from a safe distance.

    • Window seat
    • Steam sceptic
    • 7 yrs

  • Biscuit Tabby

    Kneads the dough for our cinnamon knots in spirit, and will trade a slow blink for a crumb.

    • Dough kneader
    • Slow blinks
    • 5 yrs

  • Crema Golden shaded

    Nine months old and endlessly curious about the milk jug. Naps in the pastry tray when nobody looks.

    • Milk froth fan
    • Napper
    • 9 mths

  • Espresso Scottish fold

    Sits in the darkest corner in tinted shades, judging your drink order. Secretly the softest cat here.

    • Corner booth
    • Shades on
    • 6 yrs

  • Latte Grey tabby

    Shy for the first ten minutes, then curls up on your coat. Builds forts from the reading-nook throws.

    • Fort builder
    • Lap warmer
    • 4 yrs

  • Cocoa Tabby

    A large amount of opinions and warmth. Books herself onto the sofa beside whoever looks coldest.

The menu

Espresso, single-origin chocolate, and pastry warm from the oven.

Why Catpuccino

Designed for slow afternoons, not for queues.

We keep the room quiet, the tables generous and the cats in charge.

Ninety-minute tables

Every booking comes with time to spare. Nobody is asked to hurry their espresso or their cat cuddle.

Roasted, never rushed

Small batches roasted on Mondays, rested for six days, then dialled in twice a day on the bar.

Adopt, don't shop

All twelve residents are rescue cats. Our adoption Sundays have rehomed 46 cats since 2021.

Quiet hours daily

3–5pm is screen-free and speaker-free, with low lighting and a shelf of second-hand paperbacks.

Kind words
